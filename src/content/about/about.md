title: "About Ryan Yu"
---

I am a rising senior in Chemical Engineering (Computational) Caltech. I have worked on three different research projects, spanning from atomistic simulation to AI for science. In the Goddard lab, I investigated the activation mechanism of the Smoothened GPCR. In the Fong lab, I trained machine learning potentials from scratch from self-generated DFT data to understand nitrate properties at the interfacial environment in electrochemical nitrate reduction. In the Rotskoff lab, I trained sparse autoencoders on BioEmu and ESM3 to identify what information is encoded into the latent dimensions of these models, with equilibrium thermodynamic information being of particular interest.
